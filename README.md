# GameEngiGroup
Fix All Shop (DEV 0.8)

--HOW TO PLAY--  
You are open for business! Customers will be arriving at your doorstep  
requesting your unique magical abilities.  
  
Find which spell to cast by looking at the SPELLBOOK.  
Increase mana to cast spells by looking at the ORB.  
Cast your spells by talking to a customer at the DOOR.  
  
Happy customers will increase the timer, and angry ones will decrease it. Be careful!  

--CONTROLS--  
[Click - Menu Interactions]  
[E - Go to Game Scene]  
[Escape - Pause]  
  
Door Controls:  
[WASD - Cast Direction]  
[Click - Advance Dialogue]  
  
Debug Controls:
[WASD - Movement (in Place Mode)]  
[Space - Toggle Place Mode]
